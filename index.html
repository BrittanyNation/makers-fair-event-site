Event Website UI • Original Portfolio Work

One weekend. 120 makers. Everything made by hand.

Solstice Makers Fair is a fictional two-day craft fair in a converted warehouse hall. This concept designs its website around the three questions every fair visitor actually has — who will be there, what happens when, and what does a pass cost — and answers all three without making anyone dig.

Shop

Two halls, a printed stall map at the door, and maker cards online that all carry stall numbers — so a saved list becomes a walking route, not a scavenger hunt.

Make

Eighteen workshops, each listed with its level, length, and seats left before anyone registers. Beginner sessions are labeled in words, never just by color.

Eat & listen

A food-truck court in the courtyard and local musicians on the small stage between demos — scheduled on the same page as everything else, not a separate afterthought.

Design notes

Three questions, three pages

Home answers “who and why,” Schedule answers “what happens when,” and Tickets answers “what does it cost” — one job per page, with the date, hall, and hours repeated in the hero so no page depends on another.

Plan-ability over hype

Stall numbers, payment notes, workshop levels, and seats-left counts are treated as design content, not fine print — an event site succeeds when a visitor can plan a day from it.

Festive, still readable

Deep berry carries the structure, raspberry carries the actions, and periwinkle cools the workshop and info accents — badges and selected states always pair color with words, never color alone.
