Getting to The Foundry Hall

Sample venue, fictional address: 400 Foundry Row. Free parking in the north lot, bike racks at the Hall B door, and the Route 6 bus stops at the corner every 20 minutes on weekends.

Both halls are on one level with step-free entry, wide aisles kept clear by a stated stall-layout rule, and a quiet room off Hall A open all day — because a fair this busy should still work for visitors who need a break from it.

Access notes

Stated before tickets, not after:

Step-free entry · Accessible restrooms in both halls · Quiet room in Hall A · Service animals welcome · Large-print stall maps at the door · Kids under 12 free with an adult pass.
