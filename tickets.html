Before you buy

Can I come and go during the day?

Yes — every pass includes same-day re-entry. You will get a hand stamp at the door; the stamp, not a receipt, is what gets you back in.

What if a workshop sells out?

The workshop cards on the Schedule page show seats left and are updated with the listing, so a sold-out session reads “Sold out” on the card itself instead of failing at checkout.

Are passes refundable?

In this concept, passes are refundable up to seven days before the fair and transferable any time — the terms sit on this page, in plain words, before the order form.

Solstice Makers Fair is a fictional event. This is an original portfolio concept by Brittany Nation — no affiliation with any real fair, venue, or maker is claimed, and this site sells no real tickets. Sample dates, prices, makers, and figures throughout.
